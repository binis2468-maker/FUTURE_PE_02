# UGC Ad Prompt System

## Product Brief

### Product Name
EcoGlow Smart Water Bottle

### Product Type
Reusable smart water bottle

### Target Audience
- College students
- Young professionals
- Fitness-conscious customers

### Product Benefits
- Helps users track their daily water intake
- Reusable and eco-friendly
- Displays water temperature
- Leak-proof design
- Modern and convenient for everyday use

### Brand Personality
- Modern
- Friendly
- Authentic
- Practical

### Primary Marketing Goal
Create engaging UGC-style advertising content that feels authentic and relatable while encouraging potential customers to consider and purchase the product.

### Content Platforms
- Instagram Reels
- Facebook
- YouTube Shorts

### Content Style
The advertisements should feel like genuine user-created content rather than traditional corporate advertisements. The messaging should focus on relatable problems, personal experiences, product benefits, and clear calls to action.

## Prompt 1: UGC Hook Generator

### Prompt Logic
The prompt is designed to generate multiple scroll-stopping hooks for the same product while maintaining an authentic UGC tone.

### Prompt

Act as an experienced UGC content strategist and short-form advertising copywriter.

Using the product brief above, generate 10 unique UGC-style advertising hooks for the EcoGlow Smart Water Bottle.

Target audience:
- College students
- Young professionals
- Fitness-conscious customers

Requirements:
1. Each hook should be short, attention-grabbing, and suitable for the first 2–3 seconds of a short-form video.
2. Make the hooks sound natural and conversational, as if they were spoken by a real customer.
3. Use different hook approaches, including:
   - Relatable problem
   - Personal experience
   - Curiosity
   - Unexpected discovery
   - Before-and-after situation
   - Common mistake
   - Product benefit
4. Avoid exaggerated or unrealistic claims.
5. Avoid overly corporate or promotional language.
6. Keep the language simple and easy to understand.
7. Make each hook clearly different from the others.
8. The hooks should encourage viewers to continue watching.
